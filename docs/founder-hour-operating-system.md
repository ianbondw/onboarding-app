# Founder Hour Operating System

Use this when you only have one hour a day and want Marengo to keep moving.

## Goal

Spend the hour on the highest-leverage commercial moves:

- activate existing interest
- create one new piece of demand
- remove one blocker to getting paid

## Daily Cadence

### Minute 0-10

- Open `/admin/growth`
- Check:
  - leads in the last 7 days
  - top source and campaign
  - stale new leads older than 2 days

### Minute 10-30

- Follow up with the oldest unactivated leads first
- Send them to:
  - `/demo` if they have not seen the product yet
  - `/pricing` if they are clearly qualified
  - `/pilot?plan=guided-launch` if they are ready for a live workspace

## Minute 30-45

- Publish one outbound asset:
  - one LinkedIn post
  - one cold email batch
  - one Google ad tweak

Pick only one channel per day.

## Minute 45-55

- Remove one blocker:
  - Stripe payment links
  - booking link
  - Search Console verification
  - Google Ads verification
  - legal or diligence copy

## Minute 55-60

- Write down the only metric that matters tomorrow:
  - new trial requests
  - activated trials
  - booked calls
  - direct checkouts

## Channel Rotation

- Monday: follow-ups + LinkedIn
- Tuesday: follow-ups + Google Ads
- Wednesday: follow-ups + LinkedIn
- Thursday: follow-ups + cold email
- Friday: follow-ups + offer/pricing review

## Do Not Do

- do not redesign pages without a conversion reason
- do not add broad new features unless a real prospect asked for them
- do not spend the hour reading competitors
- do not spread across more than one acquisition channel per day

## What Codex Should Own

- site copy and CTA tightening
- landing page iteration
- trust and compliance content updates
- launch docs and operator docs
- growth dashboard improvements
- scripts, automations, and checklists

## What You Still Need To Own

- Stripe account setup
- Google Ads account setup
- Search Console verification
- LinkedIn posting from your real account
- live prospect conversations and closes
