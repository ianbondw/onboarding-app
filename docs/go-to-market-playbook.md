# Marengo Go-To-Market Playbook

## Positioning

- Sell the outcome, not the software: "Modern client onboarding for RIAs with advisor workspaces, review controls, and a trust story that holds up in diligence."
- Default offer: `Guided Launch` for solo advisors and small RIAs.
- Expansion offer: `Growth Team` for multi-advisor firms that need shared review and cleaner ops visibility.

## Core Funnel

1. Cold or broad traffic goes to `/demo`.
2. Warm or high-intent search traffic goes to `/ria-onboarding-software`.
3. Price-sensitive buyers go to `/pricing`.
4. Qualified buyers go to `/pilot?plan=guided-launch`.
5. Higher-touch conversations go to `/contact`.

## Best Channels First

- Google Search for exact-intent terms:
  - `ria onboarding software`
  - `advisor onboarding software`
  - `wealth management client onboarding`
  - `client onboarding for RIAs`
- LinkedIn organic from the founder account:
  - short demo clips
  - before/after ops posts
  - trust/compliance posts
  - customer-problem posts about PDFs, email chains, and ops cleanup
- Direct outreach:
  - RIAs with 1-10 advisors
  - firms already marketing actively
  - firms whose websites look modern enough to care about onboarding UX

## Initial Google Ads Structure

- Campaign 1: `RIA onboarding software`
- Campaign 2: `advisor onboarding software`

- Use the `/ria-onboarding-software` page as the primary landing page.

## Metrics That Matter

- Homepage -> demo click rate
- Demo -> pricing click rate
- Demo -> trial creation rate
- Pricing -> trial creation rate
- Trial created -> onboarding completed
- Trial created -> advisor login
- Trial created -> paid checkout or booked call

## What Not To Do

- Do not send cold traffic straight to the trial page.
- Do not buy broad social traffic before search and founder-led LinkedIn are working.
- Do not oversell custom integrations before the core rollout package is validated.
- Do not promise enterprise implementation timelines to small teams.
